3. Information We Collect

We do not collect, receive, store or transmit any personal information from the application. There is no account system, no registration, no login, no analytics service and no crash reporting service.

The application stores only what it needs in order to work, and it stores it locally on your own device:

  • The files and documents you choose to keep in the vault.
  • The names, folders and any labels you give to those items.
  • Your interface preferences, such as sort order and theme.

This information stays in the application's private storage area on your device. It is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the application removes it. Note that if you have enabled device backup, your operating system may include application data in your own backup; that backup is controlled by you and by your platform provider, not by us.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • The contents, names or metadata of the files you keep in the vault.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your contacts, calendar or messages.
  • Your location.
  • Your device identifiers or advertising identifier.
  • Any account credentials, because the application has no accounts.
  • Usage statistics, event logs or diagnostic reports.

5. Your Files

Files you add to Arkivo are handled only on your device. They are not uploaded, mirrored, indexed remotely, scanned by us, or shared with any third party.

You are responsible for keeping your own backups. The application does not synchronise your vault to any server and cannot recover its contents for you. If you delete an item, uninstall the application, or lose or reset the device, the data held in the vault is gone and neither you nor we can restore it.

6. On-Device Processing

Everything the application does – adding items, organising them, searching, renaming and deleting – happens on your device. No part of it contacts a server.

The application works with no network connection at all.

7. Permissions

The application requests no runtime permissions. It does not ask for access to your contacts, your location, your camera, your microphone or your photo library.

When you add a file, the application uses your operating system's own file picker. You choose each item explicitly, and the application receives only what you selected. It does not browse your storage on its own and it does not request broad file-system access.

8. Network Access

The application does not make network requests. There is no advertising, no telemetry, no remote configuration and no update check of our own.

Distribution and updates are handled by the app store you installed from, according to that store's own terms and privacy policy.

9. Third-Party Services

The application contains no third-party advertising network, no analytics service, no attribution service, no social login and no crash-reporting service.

The only third parties involved are the app stores that distribute the application, and the operating system on your device. Their handling of your information is governed by their own policies.

10. Children's Privacy

The application is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed to children and it is not designed for or targeted at children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we hold no children's data. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in some way, please contact us at fbstudiosapp@gmail.com so that we can address it.

11. Data Security

Because we do not operate a server and do not collect your data, there is no developer-side database that could be breached and expose your information.

The vault is stored in the private application storage area that your operating system provides, which is protected from other applications by the operating system's sandbox on a device that has not been modified, rooted or jailbroken.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. The security of your device, including its lock screen, its operating system updates and any other software you install, is outside our control.

12. Data Retention

  • Vault contents and preferences: kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the application.
  • Data held by us: none, because we do not collect any.

13. Your Rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal information that a company holds about you. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request.

You can exercise practical control as follows:

  • Delete individual items from within the application.
  • Remove everything the application has stored by clearing its data in your device settings, or by uninstalling it.
  • Ask a question about this policy by writing to fbstudiosapp@gmail.com.

14. International Users

The application works the same way everywhere: all processing is local to your device.

Because we do not collect your information, we do not transfer your information across borders.

It is your responsibility to ensure that your use of the application complies with the laws that apply to you.
