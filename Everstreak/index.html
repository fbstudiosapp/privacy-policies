Short version: Everstreak is an offline habit tracker. Your habits, notes, streaks and statistics are stored only on your own device. FB COMPANY does not operate any server or account system and never receives a copy of your habit data. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (purchases), and only when those features are actually used.

1 About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Everstreak – Habit Tracker ("Everstreak", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. It applies to the Android version of the app distributed through Google Play by FB COMPANY.

By installing or using Everstreak you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, please uninstall the app.

2 The app works primarily offline

Everstreak was designed as a local-first, offline application:

  • There is no user account, no sign-up, no login and no profile.
  • There is no FB COMPANY server, no cloud backend and no remote database.
  • Your habits and progress are never uploaded to us or synchronised between devices.
  • All core features — creating habits, daily check-ins, streaks, statistics, history, reminders, themes and languages — work with the network completely turned off.

An internet connection is only used for two optional purposes: loading advertisements (AdMob) and processing an in-app purchase (Google Play Billing). Both are described below.

Backups you create yourself

Everstreak lets you export a backup file (JSON) of your habits and history. When you choose to export or share that file, it leaves the app's private area and is handled by whichever app or storage location you select. You are responsible for the file after that point. We never receive it.

Android system backup

If you have enabled Android Auto Backup / Google One backup in your device settings, Android may include the app's local files in your personal Google account backup. That backup is controlled by Google and by your own device settings, not by FB COMPANY. You can disable it in your Android system settings.

4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Everstreak shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Ads are limited to a bottom banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen interstitial and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a temporary feature unlock.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID or an app set ID)
  • Approximate, coarse location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language, screen size)
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, ad performance and crash diagnostics of the SDK)

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller/processor under its own terms. Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Advertising Policies for AdMob

Your advertising choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where required, a Google-certified consent message (Google UMP / CMP) is shown before personalised ads are requested. You may change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options inside the app.
  • Device controls: Android lets you opt out of ad personalisation or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Remove ads: purchasing "Remove Ads" or "Premium Lifetime" disables all advertising in the app permanently.

5 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Everstreak offers optional one-time purchases (for example "Remove Ads" and "Premium Lifetime") and an optional subscription. All purchases are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing.

  • FB COMPANY never sees or stores your payment card, bank details or billing address.
  • The app only receives a purchase token and product status from Google Play, which it uses locally to unlock features. A single boolean "premium unlocked" flag is stored on your device.
  • Your purchase is tied to your Google account, so it can be restored with the Restore purchases button after reinstalling or changing devices.
  • Refunds, payment history and subscription cancellation are managed by Google Play, not by the app.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

8 Data retention and deletion

Because everything is local, you are always in full control:

  • Delete a single item: delete an individual habit, note or check-in inside the app.
  • Delete everything: open Settings → Data → Reset all data. This permanently erases the local database, all history and all settings.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes its private storage, including the database and preferences, from your device.

Because we never receive your habit data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no deletion request is necessary. Data held by Google in connection with ads or purchases is governed by Google's own retention rules; use the Google links in sections 4 and 5 to manage it.

9 Children's privacy

Everstreak is a general-audience productivity app intended for users aged 13 and over (or the minimum age required by local law, whichever is higher). It is not directed to children and is not listed in a Google Play Families programme.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for a name, age, email address, photo or contact details. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that requires our attention, contact us at fbstudiosapp@gmail.com; because the data never leaves the device, deleting it is done by uninstalling the app or using Settings → Data → Reset all data.

10 Security

Your data resides in the app's private, sandboxed storage, which is protected by the Android operating system and — on modern devices — by full-disk or file-based encryption. All network calls made by the Google SDKs use HTTPS. Please note that no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and keeping your device updated and screen-locked remains an important part of protecting your information.

11 Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.

Everstreak supports these rights directly in the product: your data is on your device, you can view and edit it at any time, export it as a portable JSON backup, and erase it completely. FB COMPANY does not process personal data on its own servers, does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the AdMob configuration described in section 4. For rights concerning data processed by Google, please contact Google using the links above.
