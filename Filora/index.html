3. On-device file processing

Every conversion runs entirely on your device. Filora contains no upload feature, no conversion server, no cloud API integration and no remote processing of any kind. Your documents and photos are never transmitted to FB COMPANY or to any third party.

Filora performs its work using the conversion engine that ships inside the app together with standard Android graphics and PDF interfaces provided by your operating system:

  • PDF processing. Pages are rasterised locally using Android's built-in PDF renderer to produce JPG or PNG images.
  • Image processing. JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP and HEIC/HEIF files are decoded locally using Android's image decoder, then re-encoded to the format you selected.
  • HEIC/HEIF conversion. Decoding uses the HEIF support built into Android. No online service is contacted.
  • Image to PDF. PDF files are assembled locally by Filora's own PDF writer.
  • Quality, resizing and page selection. All of these options are applied locally, in memory, while the file is being converted.

4. Files you select

Filora only accesses the specific files you choose. Selection happens through the Android Storage Access Framework system file picker, which grants Filora access to the individual files you picked and to nothing else. Filora does not browse, index or scan your storage, your photo library or any file you have not explicitly selected.

Permissions

Filora requests the minimum possible set of Android permissions:

PermissionWhy it is needed

Filora does not request broad storage permissions. In particular it does not request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, calendar, phone state or installed app list.

5. Where results are stored

Converted files are written to Filora's own private application directory on your device. Other apps cannot read that directory, and uninstalling Filora removes it along with everything inside it.

A converted file only leaves that private directory when you explicitly ask for it:

  • Save to device. Filora copies the file into your shared Pictures/Filora or Download/Filora folder through Android's MediaStore, so you can find it in your gallery or file manager.
  • Share. Filora hands the receiving app you choose a temporary, read-only reference to that single file. You decide which app receives it, and that app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Open. Filora asks Android to open the file in a viewer you choose, again using a temporary read-only reference.

6. Temporary files

While preparing a conversion, the Android file picker may place a working copy of your selected file in Filora's cache directory. Filora clears its cache after a conversion finishes and when you use Clear cache in Settings. Android may also clear app caches on its own when storage runs low.

7. Conversion history stored locally

So you can find recent results again, Filora keeps a short history on your device. This history is stored in a plain file inside Filora's private application-support directory and contains only:

  • the name of the tool used (for example “PDF to JPG”),
  • the date and time of the conversion,
  • the file names, sizes, dimensions or page counts, and on-device paths of the results.

It does not contain file contents, thumbnails, or the contents of your original documents. The history is never uploaded, never synchronised and never backed up to the cloud - Filora disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its own data.

You are in control of this history. On the History screen you can delete an individual record together with its files, or clear the entire history at once. Uninstalling Filora deletes it permanently.

8. No account, no backend

Filora has no sign-up, no login, no user profile and no user accounts. We operate no server, no cloud database and no backend that receives your data. We do not know who you are, and we cannot identify you.

9. Advertising - Google AdMob

The free version of Filora shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Ads are the only reason Filora needs an internet connection for its own functionality.

Google is an independent third party and acts as its own data controller for the data it collects through its SDK. To serve, measure and limit the frequency of ads, Google may process:

  • an advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) or other device or app identifiers,
  • coarse location inferred from your IP address,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and app version,
  • ad interaction and diagnostic information such as impressions, clicks and crash data.

We never send your files, file names or file contents to any advertising or analytics service. Ads are ordinary banner and interstitial placements and have no access whatsoever to the material you convert.

You can review Google's practices in the Google Privacy & Terms documentation and in the AdMob help centre. Android also lets you reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, from your device settings.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, Filora uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are requested, and it respects the choice you make. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time using Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app, when that option is applicable to your region.

10. In-app purchases - Google Play Billing

Filora offers an optional paid upgrade, Filora Pro, which removes ads and unlocks higher limits. All purchases are handled by Google Play Billing.

  • Payment is processed by Google Play. FB COMPANY never sees, receives or stores your payment card details, billing address or Google account credentials.
  • Google Play provides Filora with the purchase state for its own products (for example whether a purchase is active) so entitlements can be unlocked.
  • Filora stores only a local flag on your device recording whether Pro is currently active, so the app works correctly while offline. That flag is re-checked against Google Play.
  • Subscriptions are managed entirely in your Google Play account, where you can also cancel them. Cancellation and refund handling follow Google Play's terms.

Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

13. Data retention

  • Converted files and history: retained on your device until you delete them, clear the history, or uninstall Filora. We keep no copy.
  • Cached working files: cleared after each conversion, when you clear the cache, or by Android when storage is low.
  • Preferences (theme, language, Pro flag): retained on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Data held by Google: retained according to Google's own retention policies, which we do not control.

14. Data deletion

Because we hold no server-side data about you, deletion is fully in your hands:

  • Delete individual results, or clear the whole history, from the History screen.
  • Clear cached working files from Settings.
  • Remove everything at once by uninstalling Filora, or via Android Settings → Apps → Filora → Storage → Clear storage.
  • To exercise rights against Google for data its SDKs processed, use the controls in your Google account.

15. Security

Because conversion is local, your files are never exposed to a network path where they could be intercepted. In addition:

  • Results are stored in Filora's private app directory, which the Android sandbox keeps inaccessible to other apps.
  • Files are shared only through temporary, read-only, per-file references, and only when you initiate a share or open action.
  • Filora's cloud backup and device-transfer rules exclude all of its data.
  • Filora requests no permission that would let it read files you did not select.

No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, and Filora cannot protect a file after you have exported or shared it to another app or location.
