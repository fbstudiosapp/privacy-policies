3.Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive, store or transmit any personal information from the application. The application contains no account system, no registration, no login, no analytics service and no crash reporting service operated by us.

The application does store a small amount of information locally on your own device so that it remembers how you like to use it:

  • Your generator preferences, such as password length and which character types are enabled.
  • Your passphrase preferences, such as the number of words and the separator.
  • Your interface preferences, such as theme, accent colour and language.
  • A flag indicating whether you have purchased the ad-free or premium option, and the expiry time of a temporary premium period earned by watching a rewarded advertisement.

This information stays in the application's private storage area on your device. It is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the application removes it. Note that if you have enabled Android backup on your device, Android may include application preferences in your own device backup; that backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

Separately from us, third-party services included in the application may process certain information. This is described in the Advertising, In-App Purchases and Third-Party Services sections below.

4.Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, files or messages.

5.Local Data Processing

All password and passphrase generation happens on your device, inside the application, using the operating system's cryptographically secure random number generator. No part of the generation process contacts a server.

The word list used by the passphrase generator is included inside the application, so no network request is made to produce a passphrase.

The application can generate passwords with no network connection at all. A connection is only relevant for displaying advertisements and for completing a purchase through Google Play.

6.Password Generation and Security

Generated passwords are created locally on your device. They are not sent to FB COMPANY servers. We do not operate a server that stores generated passwords.

The application shows a temporary session history so that you can look back at what you generated a moment ago. This history is kept in the device's working memory only. It is not written to storage, it is not encrypted or backed up by us, and it is discarded when the application is closed or removed from memory by the operating system.

You are responsible for safely storing any password you generate. The application does not back up, synchronise or recover passwords. If you generate a password, close the application and have not saved that password somewhere yourself, it cannot be retrieved by you or by us.

When you use the copy function, the password is placed on your device's clipboard so that you can paste it elsewhere. The clipboard is managed by Android, not by us. Other applications with clipboard access, and Android's own clipboard history, may be able to read it. The application offers an optional setting that attempts to clear the copied value from the clipboard after a short delay. This is a convenience feature and cannot be guaranteed, because the operating system controls the clipboard.

The strength figures and the estimated time-to-crack shown in the application are calculated estimates based on the size of the character set and the length of the password. They are provided for guidance and are not a guarantee of security.
