1. Summary

Pomolume is an offline focus timer. It has no user accounts, no servers of its own, and no analytics. Your timer settings and your session history stay on your device and are never transmitted to FB COMPANY. The only components that use the internet are Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (purchases), and both are optional in the sense that the timer works with no network connection at all.

2. Information we do not collect

FB COMPANY does not collect, receive, store, sell or share:

  • your name, e-mail address, phone number or any account identifier;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, calendar, photos, files or microphone input;
  • your focus sessions, statistics, streaks or timer settings;
  • any advertising identifier, on our own behalf.

Pomolume operates no backend server. There is no database, API or cloud storage controlled by FB COMPANY that holds information about you.

3. Data stored on your device

The app writes the following to its own private storage area, which other apps cannot read:

WhatWhyWhere
Timer durations, cycle length, daily goal, auto-start rules To restore your configuration on next launch Android SharedPreferences
Theme, colour palette, language, alert tone, sound and vibration switches To keep the app looking and sounding the way you set it Android SharedPreferences
Completed session history: type, start time, end time, planned length To calculate your statistics, streaks and history list A JSON file in the app’s private documents directory
Premium entitlement flag and the purchased product id So Premium keeps working while offline Android SharedPreferences
A snapshot of a running session So a session survives the app being closed or the device restarting Android SharedPreferences

All of it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings. Session history can also be deleted at any time from inside the app.

4. Permissions we request

PermissionPurposeOptional?
INTERNET Required by Google AdMob to load ads and by Google Play Billing to process purchases. The timer itself never needs it. Granted at install; ads simply fail to load without a connection.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To tell you a focus session or break has finished. Yes. You can deny it and the app still works; you will only get the in-app sound and vibration.
VIBRATE Vibration alert when a session ends. Can be switched off in Settings.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM So the completion alert arrives at the right moment while the app is in the background. Yes. Without it the alert is scheduled inexactly and may be a few minutes late.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To restore a pending session alert after the device restarts. Used only for that purpose. Pomolume does not run in the background otherwise.

Pomolume requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or phone-state permission of any kind.

5. Third-party services

5.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of Pomolume shows banner ads on the Statistics, History and Settings screens, and occasional full-screen ads after a break has ended. Ads never appear on the timer screen and never interrupt a running focus session.

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may process device and usage information such as an advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system version, coarse location derived from the IP address, and interaction with the ad. FB COMPANY does not receive this data; we only see aggregate, anonymous earnings reports.

Google’s handling of that data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and personalised advertising

You can limit ad personalisation at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or remove advertising entirely by purchasing Pomolume Premium.

5.2 Google Play Billing (purchases)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Pomolume never sees or stores your payment details, card number, billing address or Google account. The app only records a local flag meaning “Premium is unlocked on this device” together with the product identifier you bought.

5.3 No other SDKs

Pomolume contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, social-login or tracking SDK. There is no Firebase, no Facebook SDK and no third-party telemetry of any kind.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Pomolume Premium is offered in three forms:

  • Lifetime – a single, one-time payment.
  • Yearly – an auto-renewing subscription.
  • Monthly – an auto-renewing subscription.

Premium removes all advertising and unlocks additional colour themes, additional alert tones, advanced statistics and extended customisation.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel or manage a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Menu → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future charges; the current paid period is not refunded on a pro-rata basis. All refunds are handled by Google Play under Google’s refund policy. FB COMPANY cannot issue refunds directly.

7. Children’s privacy

Pomolume is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to collect from any user regardless of age.

Ad requests are configured with a maximum content rating of G (suitable for general audiences). If you believe a child has been shown inappropriate advertising, please contact us at the address below.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your history – open History and use “Clear history”, or Settings → Data → Clear session history.
  • Delete everything – uninstall the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → Pomolume → Storage → Clear data.
  • Turn off notifications – in the app’s Settings, or in Android notification settings.
  • Turn off ad personalisation – Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Remove ads – purchase Pomolume Premium.

Because FB COMPANY holds no personal data about you, there is no data for us to export, correct or erase on request. Rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the GDPR, and the equivalent rights under the CCPA/CPRA, are satisfied by the fact that we never receive your data in the first place. For data processed by Google as an independent controller, please exercise those rights directly with Google.

We do not sell or share personal information within the meaning of the CCPA/CPRA.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. Session history is capped at the most recent 3,000 entries; older entries are removed automatically. Nothing is retained on any server, so there is no server-side retention period.

10. Security

App data is written to Android’s private per-app storage, which is isolated from other applications by the operating system. Because no data is transmitted to us, there is no transmission for an attacker to intercept. Please note that rooting or otherwise modifying your device can weaken these operating-system guarantees.
