Privacy at a glance: ScaleNest resizes selected photos on your device. It does not require an account and does not upload photo content to an FB COMPANY image-processing server. The free version uses Google Mobile Ads, and purchases are handled by the applicable app store.

1. Who operates ScaleNest

ScaleNest: Bulk Photo Resizer is published by FB COMPANY. Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to the support address fbstudiosapp@gmail.com.

2. Photos and on-device processing

ScaleNest can access only the photos you explicitly select through the operating system's picker. Selected photos are decoded, oriented, resized, and encoded on your device. Photo content is not uploaded to FB COMPANY, Google Mobile Ads, or a remote image-processing service.

Resized working copies are written to the app's private storage. You decide whether to save copies to the device gallery or share them through the operating system's share sheet. A destination you choose from the share sheet processes the files under its own terms and privacy policy.

3. Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • approximate location inferred from an IP address;
  • app interactions, including interactions with ads;
  • diagnostic information; and
  • device or other identifiers, which may include advertising identifiers.

Google may use this information for advertising or marketing, analytics and measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. ScaleNest does not provide selected photo content to the advertising SDK.

ScaleNest requests consent where required and displays an Ad privacy choices control in Settings when Google reports that privacy options are available. Some processing required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance may not depend on advertising consent. ScaleNest Pro removes in-app advertising.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Google Play or Apple's App Store processes ScaleNest Pro purchases and subscriptions. ScaleNest receives product identifiers, purchase status, and store verification data needed to deliver or restore paid features. FB COMPANY does not receive payment-card details.

ScaleNest stores the active product identifier and entitlement state in local app preferences so paid features can remain available on the device. Purchase availability, billing, cancellation, refunds, and store transaction records are governed by the applicable store.

5. Settings stored on the device

Theme, language, advertising-frequency state, and purchase-entitlement state are stored locally in platform preferences. FB COMPANY does not receive these local settings through a proprietary server.

6. Permissions and network access

  • Photo selection: used only for photos you choose through the system picker. ScaleNest does not request broad Android photo-library read access.
  • Gallery write access: used only when you request gallery export. Legacy Android write access is limited to Android 10 and earlier.
  • Network access: used by Google Mobile Ads, consent messaging, and app-store billing. Photo resizing itself remains on-device.
  • iOS tracking permission: the operating system may request permission for advertising measurement or personalization. Refusing does not disable local photo resizing.

7. Retention and deletion

Temporary resized files remain in ScaleNest's private storage until you clear them in Settings, begin another batch that replaces them, clear the app's data through the operating system, or uninstall the app. Gallery copies must be deleted from the gallery by you. Local preferences can be removed through the operating system's app-data controls or by uninstalling ScaleNest.

ScaleNest has no user-account database and therefore has no server-side account record for FB COMPANY to delete. Google and the app stores retain information under their own policies and controls.

8. Support communications

If you email FB COMPANY, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as reasonably necessary for support, security, recordkeeping, or legal obligations. We do not use support messages to sell photo content or build advertising profiles.

9. Your choices

  • Choose which photos ScaleNest can process through the system picker.
  • Choose whether to save or share resized output.
  • Open Ad privacy choices in Settings when it is available.
  • Use operating-system controls to limit advertising identifiers or tracking.
  • Clear temporary files in ScaleNest Settings, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Contact FB COMPANY at the support address above with a privacy question.

10. Children

ScaleNest is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. The intended store audience is ages 13 and older.

11. Security

Photo processing occurs locally, Android backups and cleartext traffic are disabled by the app, and network transactions use security controls supplied by Google Mobile Ads and the app stores. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
