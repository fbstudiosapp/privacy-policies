1. Summary

StreakMint is a local-first habit tracker. It has no accounts and no sign-in. The goals, targets and check-in history you enter are written to your device's own app storage and are not uploaded to any server operated by FB COMPANY.

FB COMPANY does not operate a backend for StreakMint, does not build advertising profiles, and does not sell personal data. The only personal data processing that leaves your device happens inside Google's advertising and billing services, described in sections 4 to 6.

2. Who is responsible

This policy explains how FB COMPANY ("we", "us"), the developer and publisher of the StreakMint mobile application ("the app"), handles information in connection with the app. For any privacy question, request or complaint, write to fbstudiosapp@gmail.com.

Where data protection law applies, FB COMPANY acts as the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. Google acts as an independent controller or as a separate processor for its own advertising, billing and platform services.

3. Data you create stays on your device

Everything you enter while using StreakMint is stored locally in the app's private storage area, using the operating system's standard app preferences storage:

  • Goal names, goal types, daily targets, units and colours you choose.
  • Your check-ins and the resulting streak, completion and history values.
  • Quit-goal details you optionally enter, such as a start date, previous cigarettes per day, pack price and cigarettes per pack, which are used only to calculate on-screen figures like time elapsed, avoided items and estimated savings.
  • App settings: theme preference, language preference, a locally cached Premium flag, and the expiry time of a rewarded-ad insights preview.

We have no access to this content. It is not transmitted to us, it is not backed up to our systems, and no account is created for you. If the operating system or platform backup service (for example a device-level cloud backup you enabled yourself) copies app storage, that copy is governed by your device platform's own terms, not by us.

StreakMint includes two Google services. They may process limited technical data when you use the app:

5. Advertising and your consent choices

The free version of StreakMint displays a banner ad and offers an optional rewarded ad that temporarily unlocks 30-day insights. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

On first launch, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to determine whether a consent message is required and, if so, to show it before any ad is requested. Your choice controls whether Google may use your advertising identifier for personalised advertising. If you do not give consent where consent is required, the app will not request ads.

Changing your mind

  • In the app, open Settings → Manage ad privacy choices to reopen Google's privacy options form, where it is available for your region.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings for Google services, which limits ad personalisation across all apps.
  • Buying any Premium plan removes banner ads from the app.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described in Google's partner policy and in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

StreakMint Premium is offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the app store on your device (Google Play, or the App Store on iOS). We never see or receive your card number, billing address or full payment details.

The store tells the app only whether a valid purchase exists. The app stores a single local flag so your Premium features stay unlocked between launches, and offers a "Restore purchases" action that asks the store again. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your store account settings; the store shows the exact price and renewal terms before you confirm.

10. Retention and deleting your data

Because your habit data lives on your device, you stay in control of it:

  • Delete individual goals from the goal detail screen; their history is removed with them.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Reset local data, which clears all goals and check-in history from the device.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its entire local storage, including your settings and cached Premium flag.

FB COMPANY holds no copy of your habit data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data held by Google in connection with ads and purchases is retained under Google's own retention schedules; purchase records are kept by the store as required for tax and accounting purposes. Support emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

11. Children

StreakMint is not directed to children. It is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, whichever is higher), and some content, such as quit-smoking tracking, is aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact fbstudiosapp@gmail.com and we will act promptly.

12. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage area, which the operating system isolates from other apps. Network traffic to the ad and billing services uses encrypted connections, and the app disables clear-text HTTP traffic. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; keeping your device updated and screen-locked is the most effective protection for local data.

13. International data transfers

Google may process advertising and billing data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Google states that it relies on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, for such transfers. FB COMPANY itself does not transfer your habit data anywhere, because it never leaves your device.

Residents of California and similar jurisdictions also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: your habit data is accessible on screen, editable, and deletable at any time (section 10), and your advertising consent can be changed at any time (section 5). For anything else, or to ask about data held by us in support correspondence, email fbstudiosapp@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. For data held by Google, you can also contact Google directly using the links in section 5.
