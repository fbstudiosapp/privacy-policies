1. Application overview

Talkeeper is an offline-first Android voice recorder with speech-to-text. With Talkeeper you can record, pause, resume and stop audio; play back, rename, delete and share recordings; dictate text in real time using live speech recognition; convert an existing recording into text where the device supports it; and edit, copy, share, search and delete the resulting transcripts.

Talkeeper has no user accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no backend server operated by FB COMPANY. Your recordings, your transcripts and your settings are created and stored on your own device.

This policy explains what stays on your device, and which third-party components (Google AdMob, Google Play Billing and the speech recognition service that is part of your device’s operating system) may process data when you use the corresponding features.

2. Short summary

3. Microphone access

The microphone permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) is required for Talkeeper’s core purpose: capturing audio and converting speech into text. Without it the app cannot record and cannot dictate.

When it is requested

  • The permission is not requested when you open the app.
  • It is requested the first time you tap Start recording, start a live speech-to-text session, or transcribe an existing recording.
  • Before the system dialog appears, Talkeeper shows a short explanation of why the microphone is needed.

How it is used

  • The microphone is only active during an explicit recording or recognition session that you started.
  • There is no background listening, no always-on trigger word and no hidden capture. Talkeeper does not run a background recording service.
  • The recording screen always shows the current state (ready, recording, paused) and the elapsed time.
  • You can revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Talkeeper › Permissions. Recording and dictation stop working, and the rest of the app (playback, transcripts, search) keeps working.

4. Audio recordings

Every recording you make is written to a file inside Talkeeper’s own private application directory. Depending on the quality preset you select, the format is AAC (.m4a) or uncompressed WAV (.wav).

Alongside each audio file, Talkeeper stores a small local index entry containing:

  • the file name and the title you gave the recording,
  • the creation date and time, the duration and the file size,
  • technical audio settings (sample rate, bit rate, number of channels),
  • a reference to the transcript that belongs to the recording, if there is one,
  • whether you marked the recording as a favourite.

This index is a plain local file. It is never sent anywhere. Renaming a recording renames the local file; deleting a recording deletes the audio file and its transcript.

Sharing is always your decision. A recording leaves the app only when you tap Share and then choose a target application yourself (for example email, a messenger or a cloud drive). From that moment the receiving app’s own privacy policy applies to the copy you sent. Talkeeper cannot control or withdraw content you have shared.

5. Speech recognition and speech-to-text processing

Talkeeper does not contain its own speech recognition engine. It uses the standard Android speech recognition interface, which is served by the recognition service installed on your device. On most Android phones this is a Google component (for example the Google app or Speech Recognition & Synthesis).

Where the processing happens

This is decided by your device and its configuration, not by Talkeeper:

  • On-device (offline) recognition happens when the language pack for the selected recognition language has been downloaded on your device and the system recogniser chooses to work locally. In that case the audio does not leave the device.
  • Network-assisted recognition happens when the system recogniser decides to use an online model. In that case the audio captured during the recognition session is transmitted to and processed by the provider of that recognition service (normally Google) under their privacy policy, not this one.

Talkeeper cannot force or guarantee offline recognition on every device. To keep recognition on the device, install the offline language pack for your language in the Android system settings for speech and voice input. Talkeeper’s recognition-language screen lists exactly the languages the recogniser on your device reports as available.

Transcribing an existing recording

Android’s recogniser cannot be fed an audio file directly. When you use Transcribe this recording, Talkeeper plays the recording out loud through the device speaker and lets the on-device recogniser listen to it. The app states this clearly on that screen before you start. The audio itself is never uploaded by Talkeeper; what happens to the captured speech afterwards is governed by the recognition service, as described above.

What Talkeeper stores

Only the recognised text is stored, in Talkeeper’s local transcript index, together with the title, the creation and update timestamps, the recognition language identifier, the source (live session or from a recording) and the link to the related recording. You can edit or delete any transcript at any time.

6. Local device storage

Talkeeper writes to exactly three places, all of them local:

  • The app’s private files directory – audio recordings, the recordings index, the transcripts index, and temporary export files you create with the export/share actions.
  • The app’s preferences store – your settings (theme, app language, recording quality, recognition language, recogniser options) and the cached list of purchases Google Play reports for your account.
  • Files you deliberately export or share – these are handed to the app you choose in the Android share sheet.

The private application directory is not readable by other apps, needs no storage permission, and is removed by Android when Talkeeper is uninstalled. Talkeeper does not request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or any READ_MEDIA_* permission, and it does not scan your photo, video or music library.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Talkeeper’s data, so your recordings are not copied into a Google backup of your device.

7. Are recordings uploaded?

No. Talkeeper never uploads your audio files or transcripts to FB COMPANY or to any server operated by FB COMPANY. There is no FB COMPANY backend, no analytics service and no crash-reporting service in this app.

To be accurate and complete, there are exactly two situations where content can leave the device:

  1. You share or export it. You tap Share or Export and pick a destination app yourself.
  2. Your device’s speech recogniser uses an online model. As explained in section 5, the audio of a recognition session may then be processed by the provider of that recognition service (normally Google).

10. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of Talkeeper is funded by advertising delivered through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ad formats used are a banner at the bottom of list screens, one native ad card inside the recordings list, and an occasional full-screen interstitial.

How ads are placed

  • No ads are shown on the recording screen, and no full-screen ad can appear while audio is being recorded or while a speech recognition session is running.
  • Interstitials appear only at natural break points, such as after a transcript has been saved, and are rate limited.
  • Ads are labelled as advertising and never disguised as app content or as a system message.

What the ads SDK may process

To serve, measure and cap ads, Google and its ad partners may process technical and usage data such as:

  • the advertising identifier of the device (or an equivalent identifier),
  • IP address and derived coarse location (for example country or region),
  • device model, operating system version, language and screen characteristics,
  • ad interaction events (an ad was requested, shown, clicked or dismissed).

This processing happens inside the Google Mobile Ads SDK. FB COMPANY does not receive this data in an identifiable form; it only sees aggregated earnings and performance reports in the AdMob console. For details see the Google Privacy & Terms: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the AdMob help centre.

Consent

Talkeeper integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). If you are in a region that requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or certain US states), a consent message is shown before personalised ads are requested, and you can change your choice later at any time through Settings › Ad privacy settings (this entry appears only where a privacy options form is required). If you withhold consent, ads may still be shown in a non-personalised form.

Removing ads

Buying Remove ads or any Talkeeper Pro product disables the ad SDK entirely: no further ad requests are made from the app.

11. Advertising identifier

Talkeeper declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission because the Google Mobile Ads SDK uses the Android advertising ID for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. The advertising ID is a resettable, non-permanent identifier that is not tied to your name.

You control it on your device:

  • Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (wording varies by manufacturer) lets you reset the advertising ID.
  • The same screen lets you delete the advertising ID, after which apps can no longer read it.

Users who purchase an ad-free product are not subject to any ad-related identifier use in this app.

12. Google Play Billing and in-app purchases

Talkeeper offers optional paid upgrades. All payments are handled by Google Play Billing. Talkeeper never sees or stores your card number, bank details, billing address or Google account password.

What is offered

  • Remove ads – a one-time purchase that hides all advertising.
  • Talkeeper Pro lifetime – a one-time purchase unlocking all Pro features.
  • Talkeeper Pro monthly / yearly – auto-renewing subscriptions unlocking all Pro features.

Pro features are: no advertising, Studio and lossless WAV recording quality, transcript export as .txt and .srt, unlimited live transcription session length, variable playback speed, and bulk export tools. Every other feature — recording, pausing, resuming, stopping, playback, renaming, deleting, sharing, live speech-to-text, transcript editing and search — is available free of charge.

Transparency

  • The price shown in the app is the localised price supplied by Google Play, including the billing period for subscriptions.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not retroactively refund the current period.
  • Nothing is charged automatically and no purchase is pre-selected. There is no trial that silently converts into a paid subscription.

What Talkeeper stores about purchases

Only the product identifiers that Google Play reports as owned by the signed-in Google account, cached locally so that paid features continue to work offline. This cache contains no payment information and no account identifier. It is refreshed against Google Play each time the app starts and whenever you tap Restore purchases, which is also what removes access again after a refund or an expired subscription.

Because Talkeeper has no backend, purchases are validated against Google Play on the device itself rather than on a server. Refunds and billing disputes are handled by Google Play; see the Google Play refund help page.

17. Data deletion

Because everything is local, you can delete your data yourself at any time:

  • One recording – open the recording’s menu and choose Delete. The audio file and its transcript are removed.
  • One transcript – open the transcript and choose Delete.
  • Everything at once – Settings › Storage management offers Delete all recordings and Delete all transcripts, plus Remove orphaned files.
  • All app data – Android Settings › Apps › Talkeeper › Storage › Clear storage.
  • Uninstall – removes the app together with every recording, transcript and setting it created.

Deletion inside the app is immediate and permanent; there is no recycle bin and no remote copy to recover from. Export or share anything you want to keep before deleting it.

To ask a question about deletion, or to request deletion of support correspondence, write to fbstudiosapp@gmail.com. FB COMPANY cannot delete recordings or transcripts on your device on your behalf, because it has no access to them.

19. Security

  • Recordings, transcripts and settings live in Talkeeper’s private application directory, which the Android sandbox keeps inaccessible to other apps.
  • No credentials, tokens or personal data are transmitted by the app, because there is no account system and no FB COMPANY server.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app’s data, so recordings are not duplicated off the device by a system backup.
  • Network traffic generated by the Google SDKs uses the encrypted connections those SDKs establish.
  • The index files are written atomically, so an interrupted write cannot corrupt your library.

No security measure is absolute. Device-level protection matters: use a screen lock, keep Android and the app updated, install apps only from trusted sources, and be careful about where you share recordings. If your device is lost, unlocked or shared, the recordings on it can be accessed by whoever holds the device.

20. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection law (for example the GDPR in the EEA and the UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California) gives you rights over your personal data, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, as well as the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

How these apply here:

  • Data on your device. You exercise these rights directly. You already have full access, you can edit any transcript, you can rename or delete anything, and you can export your transcripts to .txt or .srt files for portability. FB COMPANY holds no copy and cannot act on this data.
  • Advertising data. Use the in-app Ad privacy settings entry where it is available, and the advertising ID controls in your Android settings. For requests addressed to Google as controller, use the channels in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase data. Handled by Google Play under your Google account.
  • Support correspondence. Email fbstudiosapp@gmail.com to access or delete it.

FB COMPANY does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising in the sense of the CCPA/CPRA, and does not build user profiles.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and believe your data has been handled unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.
