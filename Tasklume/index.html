Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 August 2026 · Last updated: 9 August 2026

Short version. Tasklume is an offline to-do list. Your tasks, lists, notes, due dates and settings are stored only on your own device. FB COMPANY does not operate a server for this app, has no user accounts, and cannot see your task content. The only data that leaves your device is the limited technical information that Google’s advertising and payment services need in order to work.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how FB COMPANY (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile application Tasklume – Offline To-Do List (the “App”), distributed through Google Play under the package name com.fbcompany.tasklume.

By installing or using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Data you create in the App (stored on your device only)

Everything you enter into Tasklume is written to a private storage area that belongs to the App on your device:

  • Task titles, notes, subtasks and completion history
  • Lists / categories, their names, colours and icons
  • Due dates, times, reminder times, priorities and repeat rules
  • App preferences (theme, accent colour, language, sorting, reminder settings)
  • Your purchase status for the Pro upgrade (a simple on/off flag)

This content is kept in a local SQLite database and in local key-value preferences. It is not transmitted to us, is not synchronised to any cloud, and is not shared with third parties. We have no technical ability to read it.

Android system backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this App, so your task data is not copied off the device by the operating system either. If you want a copy, use Settings → Data → Export backup, which creates a JSON file and lets you decide where to send or save it.

3. Data processed by third-party services

The App is free and supported by advertising, and it offers optional paid upgrades. To make this possible we use Google services that may process a limited amount of data. We do not send them your task content.

3.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect the quality of ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and, where applicable, an app-set ID
  • IP address and approximate, coarse location derived from it (country / region level)
  • Device and app information: model, operating system version, language, app version
  • Ad interaction events such as impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic and crash information relating to the ad SDK

Purposes: displaying advertising, measuring ad performance, fraud prevention and ad frequency control. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the App shows a Google-certified consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are requested. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → About → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in the Android system settings, or remove advertising entirely by buying the Remove ads or Pro upgrade.

3.2 Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details never reach the App or FB COMPANY. Google Play processes the transaction and returns only a purchase receipt, which the App uses to unlock the features you bought. Google Play acts as the merchant of record; see the Google Privacy Policy.

3.3 Google Play services and Google Play Console

Google may collect installation, crash and performance statistics for apps distributed through Google Play. We can view these reports only in aggregated or pseudonymised form in Play Console. This processing is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, e-mail address, phone number or sign-in.
  • We do not upload, read, sell or rent your tasks, notes or lists.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs that profile you across other apps.
  • We do not request contacts, photos, files, precise GPS location, microphone or camera access.
  • We do not build advertising profiles ourselves.

5. Permissions the App requests

PermissionWhy it is needed
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Only so Google AdMob and Google Play Billing can work. All task features work with the network switched off.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows local reminders you set yourself. Requested only when you enable a reminder or the daily agenda. Notifications are generated on the device; no push service is used.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised advertising and, where required, for storing or accessing identifiers on your device.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for non-personalised advertising, security and fraud prevention, and for keeping the App stable.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for delivering the paid features you purchase.

Because your task data never leaves your device, we are not a controller of that content: it stays under your sole control.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data stays until you delete it. You can remove individual tasks and lists, clear completed history, use Settings → Data → Delete all data, clear the app storage, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling deletes the local database and preferences.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to Google’s own retention policies.
  • Purchase records are retained by Google Play as required for billing, tax and refund purposes.

8. International transfers

We do not transfer data ourselves. Google may process the data described in section 3 on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, using the safeguards described in Google’s privacy documentation (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

9. Children’s privacy

Tasklume is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests are configured with a maximum ad content rating suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the advertising services used in the App, contact us at fbstudiosapp@gmail.com and we will help you raise the request with the relevant provider.

10. Security

Task data is stored in the App’s private sandbox, which the Android operating system isolates from other apps. Purchase verification is performed through Google Play. Please note that no method of electronic storage is completely secure; keeping your device updated and protected by a screen lock is the most effective protection for locally stored data.
