1. Introduction

FB COMPANY (“we”, “us”, “our”) develops and publishes Cutoria – Background Remover (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not process, and which third-party services are involved when you use it.

We built the App so that your photos never need to leave your device. Please read this policy carefully. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or any other directly identifying information.

FB COMPANY operates no servers that receive data from the App. We have no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no advertising identifier of our own.

The App contains two third-party SDKs, Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, which may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies. Those cases are described in the Advertising and In-App Purchases sections below.

3. Images and Background Removal Processing

The App exists to remove the background from an image you choose. That work happens entirely on your device.

  • You select an image using the Android photo picker, or capture one with your camera. The App receives only the single file you choose.
  • The image is decoded, analysed and cut out locally, using on-device machine learning models and local image processing code.
  • The resulting transparent PNG is written only where you ask: to your device gallery, or to the app you pick from the Android share sheet.
  • We never upload, transmit, copy or store your images on any server operated by FB COMPANY. There is no image server, no cloud processing and no remote background removal API.

4. Local / On-Device Processing

Background removal uses Google ML Kit segmentation models that run locally on your device, together with the App’s own local image processing code. Your image data is processed in your device’s memory and is not sent to Google or to us for analysis.

One of the models (ML Kit Subject Segmentation) is delivered to your device through Google Play services. Downloading that model may require an internet connection the first time, and it involves Google Play services requesting the model files. This download transfers the model to your device; it does not transfer your images anywhere. Once the model is present, background removal works offline. If the model is unavailable, the App falls back to a segmentation model bundled inside the App and to its own offline algorithm, both of which work with no internet connection at all.

5. Data Storage

The App stores only a small amount of data, all of it locally on your device and none of it personal:

  • Your preferences: selected language, theme, haptic feedback setting and default export resolution.
  • Whether you have completed the introductory screens.
  • A cached flag recording whether you purchased “Remove ads” or “Pro”, so premium features work immediately at launch. This flag is always re-checked against Google Play.
  • A per-day counter of how many free background removals you have used, and a lifetime count of completed exports, used only to apply the free daily limit and to pace advertisements.
  • Temporary working files in the App’s private cache directory, created while an image is being processed or shared. You can delete these at any time from Settings, and Android may clear them automatically.

There is no database on our side, because there is no server on our side.

6. Permissions

We request only what the App genuinely needs:

  • Internet access – required for advertisements, for Google Play Billing and for the one-time on-device model download. Background removal itself does not use the internet.
  • Camera – used only if you choose “Take a photo”. The photo is handled by the system camera and returned to the App as a single file.
  • Advertising ID – declared so that Google AdMob can serve advertisements in accordance with your consent choices. It is not used by us for any other purpose, and it is not read at all if you purchase an ad-free version.
  • Photo and media access – not requested. The App uses the Android photo picker, a system component that returns only the image you pick, so no broad access to your gallery is required.
  • Saving to your gallery – on Android 10 and newer this needs no permission, because the App writes through the system media store. On older Android versions a storage-write permission is requested the first time you save an image.

7. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of the App shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob is a Google service and acts as an independent controller of the data it processes.

To serve advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process technical information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system version, coarse location derived from your IP address, and interaction data about the advertisements shown. We do not receive this information and we cannot link it to you.

You can review Google’s practices in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Advertisements are placed so that they cannot be confused with app controls. Full-screen advertisements appear only after you have completed an export, never when the App starts and never while you are editing. Purchasing “Remove ads” or “Pro” disables the advertising SDK completely.

8. Advertising Identifiers and Your Consent

Where consent is required, a consent form is presented before any personalised advertising request is made.

The advertising SDK is only initialised after the consent flow reports that advertising requests are permitted. If you decline, or if the consent state cannot be established, the App does not request advertisements.

You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options, where that option is required in your region. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android system settings.

9. Analytics

The App contains no analytics SDK. We do not track your usage, we do not build profiles and we do not measure your behaviour inside the App. No usage statistics are transmitted to FB COMPANY.

Google Play may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics about installs, ratings and crashes as part of the normal Google Play Console reporting available to every developer. We cannot identify individual users from those reports.

10. In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing

The App offers optional one-time purchases: “Remove ads” and “Pro”. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive or store your payment card details, billing address or Google account credentials.

Google Play tells the App which products your Google account owns so that premium features can be unlocked and restored on a new device. We store only a local true/false flag for each product. Purchases are subject to the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s privacy policy.

Refunds are handled by Google Play according to its own policies. If you need help with a purchase, contact us at fbstudiosapp@gmail.com.
