Privacy Policy

This policy explains the data practices of the CodeNest QR Android application distributed on Google Play (the “App”). The App's developer and publisher is FB COMPANY.

1. QR and barcode data processed on the device

Camera frames, images the user selects through the system picker, scanned QR/barcode content, and generated code content are processed on the device. If automatic history is enabled, scanned or generated content may be stored with history and favorite information in the App's local database. FB COMPANY does not operate a user-account service, application server, or cloud-sync service and does not receive this content on its own servers.

The App uses ML Kit barcode scanning. According to Google, input images, video, text, and resulting outputs are processed on-device and are not sent to Google servers. Auto-zoom may suggest a camera zoom ratio to improve recognition of a distant potential barcode; it does not automatically open a link.

2. Permissions and user-initiated actions

  • Camera: Requested only to scan a code with the camera. Permission can be revoked in system settings.
  • Image selection: The App does not request broad photo or video read access. The user selects an image through the system picker.
  • Save, share, and print: A PNG, PDF, or CSV leaves the App only when the user explicitly starts a save, share, or print action. The selected destination app or service applies its own privacy terms.

3. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and UMP

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to collect and store advertising privacy choices where required. Google's current Android disclosure states that the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following data for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention, security, and compliance purposes:

  • the IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • product interactions such as app launches, taps, and ad/video views;
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy use;
  • the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

This SDK traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. The App does not request location permission, although approximate location may be inferred from an IP address. Scanned/generated code content and camera images are not supplied as custom parameters to the advertising SDK. Ad personalization and participating partners may vary by region, the user's UMP choices, and Google's configuration.

4. ML Kit technical metrics and auto-zoom

Rather than the input image or barcode result itself, ML Kit sends Google SDK performance and usage metrics. Google's current disclosure says these metrics may include:

  • device manufacturer/model, operating-system version and build, and available ML hardware accelerators;
  • application package name and version and a per-installation identifier not intended to uniquely identify a user or physical device;
  • performance metrics such as latency; API configuration such as image format and resolution; input/output size; and feature version;
  • event types such as initialization, model download, detection, and resource release, and related error codes;
  • when auto-zoom is enabled, a dynamically generated scanning-session ID not intended to uniquely identify a user or device, zoom-level changes, and predicted coordinates of a bounding box potentially containing a barcode.

Google processes these metrics for diagnostics, usage analytics, performance measurement, debugging, maintenance and improvement of the APIs, and detection of misuse or abuse. They are sent to Google using HTTPS and, according to Google's ML Kit disclosure, are not transferred to third parties.

5. Retention, backups, and deletion

Local history and favorites remain on the device until the user deletes them individually, uses Settings → Clear local data, or uninstalls the App. “Clear local data” removes saved code history and favorite states in those records; advertising privacy preferences are managed separately through “Privacy choices.” Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the App's private data.

Exported files and copies shared with other apps are no longer under the App's control; users must delete those copies at their destination. FB COMPANY does not set an independent retention period for SDK metrics sent by AdMob and ML Kit. Retention and deletion of that data are governed by Google's policies and user controls.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Where required, use Settings → Privacy choices to review or change ad consent. Turning off personalization may not stop all ads or all technical data processing.
  • You may reset or delete the advertising ID and revoke camera permission in Android settings.
  • You may delete local code records in the App's settings.
  • Send requests concerning applicable data-protection rights to fbstudiosapp@gmail.com. FB COMPANY cannot access on-device code content that was not sent to the developer.

If a user chooses to email support, the email address, message, and attachments may be retained as necessary to answer the request, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. The user may request deletion at the same email address.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is offered to a general audience. The publisher must keep the Google Play target-audience selection and advertising configuration consistent with the actual audience. This policy will be updated if the audience or data practices change.

8. Security, international processing, and changes

The App excludes its private data from Android backups and the described SDK traffic uses encrypted connections, but no method can guarantee absolute security. Google may process SDK data outside the user's country under the Google Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
