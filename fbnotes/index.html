Privacy Policy

FB Notes is a simple, offline, and ad-free note-taking application published by FB Company. This Privacy Policy explains how the FB Notes app handles user data.

Overview

FB Notes is designed to work locally on your device. The app does not require user registration, does not display ads, does not use analytics, and does not send your notes to any server.

Data Collection

FB Notes does not collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, location, advertising ID, contacts, photos, audio, messages, device identifiers, or similar personal information.

Notes and Local Storage

Notes created in FB Notes are stored locally on your device.

Your note content is not sent to the developer, to a developer server, or to any third-party service.

The developer of FB Notes cannot access, read, recover, or manage the notes stored on your device.

Internet Usage

FB Notes does not require an internet connection for its note-taking features.

The app does not communicate with developer servers and does not upload user-created notes.

Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

FB Notes does not contain ads.

The app does not use AdMob, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash tracking SDKs, or user tracking systems.

Third-Party Sharing

FB Notes does not share user data with third parties.

Data Security

Because FB Notes stores notes locally on your device and does not transmit note content to external servers, your notes remain under the control of your device.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their own device, for example by using a screen lock, password, PIN, fingerprint, or other device security features.

Android System Backup

Depending on your Android device settings and the app's backup configuration, app data may be included in Android's standard backup or device transfer processes.

If Android system backup is enabled on your device, some app data may be backed up through your Google account as part of Android's standard backup system. The developer of FB Notes cannot access those backups.

Data Retention and Deletion

FB Notes stores notes on your device until you delete them.

You can delete notes inside the app at any time.

You can also permanently remove app data by clearing the app's storage from Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.

Because FB Notes does not store your notes on developer servers, the developer cannot delete, restore, or access your notes remotely.

Children's Privacy

FB Notes is a general-purpose note-taking app and is not specifically directed to children.

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
